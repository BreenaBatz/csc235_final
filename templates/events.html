Event Space Rentals

Celebrate your story with handcrafted mead made in small batches in Coventry, Connecticut. Whether you’re planning an intimate wedding, a cozy shower, or a spirited bachelor/bachelorette gathering, Dragonfire Meadery offers a one-of-a-kind experience rooted in tradition and flavor. The Dragonfire Meadery facility can be rented for parties, special events, fundraisers and gatherings. We offer different packages based on the space needed for the event.
Arrangements must be made in advance to rent the space. Call or email for availability.

Amenities

Includes Features

All of our Private event rentals include:

  • Tables/chairs/stools for guests and food.
  • Discussion about mead and the process of making mead. (optional)
  • Meads, mead cocktails, mocktails per person based on package. (prices may vary)

PARKING: We have limited parking available on site. ( about 6 cars) so we do recommend carpooling for smaller groups. For anything more than that shuttle services can be provided at an additional fee.
